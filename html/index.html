About Us

EKKO NOW is a disruptive media-tech company at the intersection of media, technology, and climate advocacy. Our mission is to empower individuals, businesses, and communities by providing access to knowledge, opportunities, and sustainable solutions through storytelling, digital innovation, and community-driven engagement.
